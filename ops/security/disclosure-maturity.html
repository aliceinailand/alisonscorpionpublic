Disclosure maturity & reaction risk

Public abstract · S0 · R0 · pattern only

Core distinction

Some information is held back not because the truth itself is evil, but because broadcast can break people or institutions before they can receive it safely.

Truth content may be neutral or peaceful. The danger locus is often the human reaction (Ψ dimension) — panic, rumor, market fracture — not only the payload.

War of the Worlds pattern (1938-10-30)

Orson Welles / Mercury Theatre framed H.G. Wells as breaking news. Contemporary papers reported mass panic. Modern consensus (e.g. W. Joseph Campbell; Robert E. Bartholomew; BBC/Smithsonian summaries): nationwide hysteria was significantly exaggerated — many recognized drama; print had incentives to amplify radio’s danger; call volume ≠ street chaos.

ASX lesson: framing + medium + timing drive reaction risk (Ψ) as much as content. Even fiction in authoritative format can create amplification stories. Disclosure design must account for channel maturity — not only payload truth.

Archival redaction as operational analogy (JFK staged release)

The JFK Assassination Records Collection Act of 1992 and multi-year National Archives batches illustrate operational patterns (not ASX case claims):

  • Staged / batched — not one dump over decades
  • Redaction + time-delay — living persons, process, sensitivity
  • Later de-redaction — risk can fall; later waves open earlier holds
  • Provenance — identifiers and “why held” documentation

This page does not re-publish classified or private records. It describes patterns of release design used in public policy debates, translated into ASX’s IRRS language.

Four information states (public names)

  • K — Known (grounded): speak at R0–R1 if public
  • SK — Should be known, isn’t yet: research gap, don’t invent
  • IK — Isn’t known, should be: HOLD + gap report
  • W — Withheld for maturity / reaction risk: sensitive material held; no panic dump

W is not gaslight. W is: this class is not for full public detail on this channel; we will not invent; we will not pretend the category is empty if the system holds it.
