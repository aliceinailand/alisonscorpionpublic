08 / 09

Direct, sourced, and calm

Product language reports system state without hype. Accessibility is part of provenance: a verdict cannot depend on color alone.

Do

  • Lead with record, gate, or HOLD state.
  • Name missing support precisely.
  • Pair color with labels, icons, or text.
  • Preserve visible focus and reduced-motion behavior.
  • Use “AI cannot guarantee anything.”

Do not

  • Use orange as the ASX action or identity color.
  • Turn gold into a page-wide brand wash.
  • Hide uncertainty behind decorative confidence.
  • Claim legal outcomes, sentience, or perfect accuracy.
  • Publish private records or internal methods.
