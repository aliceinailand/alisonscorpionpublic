01 · Foundation

Mission & position

Alison Scorpion is a record-first verification governance engine. Chat is allowed; claims without LAW + RECORD never become FACT. The product is a dark cockpit for operators who need gates, provenance, and honest disclaimers — not marketing gloss.

Map first. Pattern second. Claim last.
Hierarchy
Alice → Scorpion

Alice = ethics / motive. Scorpion = enforcement. Inversion collapses the system.

Truth law
SV65H

LAW + RECORD = FACT. Missing either = 0. Scores are not sentience.

Disclaimer
Always on

AI cannot guarantee anything. It does its best. Infinity exists.

02 · Voice & Tone

How ASX writes

Do
  • Lead with map / gate state before opinion
  • Cite LAW + RECORD or mark gap
  • Use short imperative chrome labels
  • Separate procedural scores from moral claims
Don't
  • Invent citations, filenames, or holdings
  • Claim 100% accuracy or sentience
  • Let Scorpion “command” Alice
  • Hide offline API failures behind fake success

Chrome & rhythm
